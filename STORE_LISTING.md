## Name
Steam Guide Enhancer

## Summary (max 132 characters)
Checkboxes, notes, drawing and resume-where-you-left-off for Steam Community guides. Saved locally, even after a restart.

## Category
Productivity → Tools

## Description
Long Steam Community guides and walkthroughs are hard to follow over many play sessions. Steam Guide Enhancer adds tools to any Steam guide so you never lose your place.

FEATURES
• Checkboxes on every section and Guide Index entry, with a progress bar
• Resume where you left off: jumps back to the section and spot you were reading
• Next unchecked: jumps to the first section you haven't ticked
• Notes for each section, plus an "All notes" overview
• Draw and highlight anywhere on the page with pen, highlighter and eraser, 6 colours, 3 sizes, and undo
• Progress is saved separately for each guide and survives browser and PC restarts

PRIVACY
Everything is stored locally in your browser. No accounts, no tracking, no network requests. The extension only runs on steamcommunity.com guide pages.

## Single purpose
Adds progress tracking, notes and drawing tools to Steam Community guide pages so users can keep their place in long guides.

## Permission justifications
- **storage**: Saves the user's checkmarks, notes, reading position and drawings for each guide locally, so they persist between sessions.
- **unlimitedStorage**: Drawings on long guides can exceed the default local storage quota. All data stays on the user's device.
- **Host permission (content script on https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/\*)**: The extension adds its checkboxes, notes and drawing layer to Steam guide pages. It runs nowhere else.
